Klondike. Build the four foundations up from Ace to King by suit; stack columns down in alternating colors; only a King fills an empty column. Click a card then where it goes, or drag it. Double-click (or press F) sends a card to a foundation. U undoes. Windows-style standard scoring: +10 for each card to a foundation, +5 waste → column, +5 for turning a column card over, −15 foundation → column, −100 (draw 1) or −20 (draw 3) each time the waste goes back to the stock, −2 every 10 seconds. Winning adds 700,000 ÷ seconds played (for games over 30 seconds).
